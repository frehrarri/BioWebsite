Technical Skills

computerTechnical Skills

I have broken my experience with technologies into five distinct classifications: Novice, Adequate, Competent, Proficient, and Mastered. The way I quantify my skills should not be taken by its classification without reading the reasoning in the specific technologies' section. While there are plenty of skills that will be listed as Adequate, these should be taken with a grain of salt as I can still do meaningful work with them. It should also be noted that I feel I can pick up any technology given a short rampup period.


  • Novice means I have barely any experience with the skill - these have been omitted from this page.
  • Adequate means that I have used the technology and have understanding of its principles and I can contribute to projects, but theres plenty room for improvement.
  • Competent means that I have learned enough to be able to contibute somewhat easily, but there is still room for improvement.
  • Proficient means that I have a great understanding of the technology, can contribute easily, but I wouldn't say I have mastered it.
  • Mastery is a category I don't consider achieveable as there is always something to learn and improve on, so it is omitted.
